# Brand Tracker

> **Status: Active Development** — Core sentiment analysis is functional. Advanced analytics and AI features are in progress.

---

## The Idea

Every entrepreneur whether they're running a small local business or scaling a startup — deserves to know how the world sees their brand.

Most brand intelligence tools today are built for enterprises with big budgets and dedicated teams. The early-stage founder selling handmade products, the freelancer building a personal brand, the small business owner trying to understand why customers are leaving they're left behind.

**Brand Tracker exists to change that.**

By tracking sentiment trends from real customer feedback, Brand Tracker gives entrepreneurs at every level a clear, honest picture of how their brand is evolving — not just a snapshot, but a story over time. The goal isn't just data. It's the kind of insight that helps a founder make their next move with confidence.

> *"You can't improve what you can't see. Brand Tracker makes your brand visible to you."*

---

## What It Does

- Accepts textual brand feedback - reviews, comments, mentions
- Preprocesses and normalizes the text
- Classifies sentiment as **positive**, **negative**, or **neutral**
- Tracks how perception shifts and trends over time
- Surfaces patterns that help entrepreneurs make smarter decisions
